About Me

I am a Computer Science (Data Science) student at Ontario Tech University with a Diploma in Software Support from Mohawk College. I am passionate about technology, data, problem-solving, and finding practical ways to improve systems and processes. During my co-op at Evertz Microsystems, I analyzed system logs and configuration data, supported internal dashboards using JavaScript and JSON, troubleshot technical issues, and updated process documentation. This experience strengthened my analytical thinking, attention to detail, and ability to communicate technical information clearly. Through my academic and personal projects, I have worked with SQL, Python, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, Bash, WordPress, and Excel. I have used SQL for data validation, Excel for data analysis and visualization, Bash for basic automation, and web technologies for responsive website development. I am currently developing my skills in data analytics, Python, and Power BI through my studies and the IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate. I enjoy learning new technologies, solving challenging problems, and turning what I learn into practical solutions. I am particularly interested in data analytics, software testing, automation, technical support, and process improvement.
